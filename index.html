Why We Combine XGBoost and PGM for Loan Decisions

One model predicts risk fast. The other reasons through how a borrower's finances actually connect. Together they make decisions that are accurate and explainable.

02 PGM Relationship Reasoning

Reasoning through repayment capacity

A Probabilistic Graphical Model encodes how variables influence each other. Tap a node to trace its path through the network.

Income · Debt · Employment Stability → Repayment Capacity → Default Risk
How it differs: PGM models relationships between variables. Instead of treating income and debt independently, it reasons through repayment capacity.
