Step-by-Step Process

Laboratory Service Workflow

From your initial request to final certified report — here's exactly how your sample moves through our system, transparently and traceably.

📋
Step 1
Customer Submits Request
Customer fills in the service form specifying the test type or COA report needed.
🔍
Step 2
Admin Reviews Request
Admin verifies the submitted form and confirms all required information is complete.
🧾
Step 3
Invoice Issued & Payment
Cashier generates the invoice and customer completes payment to proceed.
🧪
Step 4
Sample Prepared & Registered
Admin prepares and logs the physical sample with a unique tracking ID.
⚗️
Step 5
Lab Testing & Data Recording
The laboratory conducts testing and records all raw data per ISO/IEC 17025 standards.
🗄️
Step 6
Data Stored Securely
All test data is securely stored in the centralized system for full audit traceability.
🖥️
Step 7
COA Report Generated
The IT system automatically generates the Certificate of Analysis (COA) from stored data.
✅
Step 8
Lab Verifies the Report
A qualified laboratory officer reviews and approves the COA for accuracy and compliance.
📨
Step 9
Report Stamped & Delivered
The final COA is officially stamped, scanned, and delivered directly to the customer.

Our Facilities

Specialized Laboratories

STINL comprises six purpose-built laboratory divisions, each equipped to deliver specialized testing and analysis to international standards.

MBL
Microbiology Laboratory
Focus: Microorganism Testing
  • Detection of bacteria and pathogens
  • Food safety testing
  • Water microbiology
  • Public health monitoring
⚕ Prevent disease and contamination risks
FCL
Food Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Chemical Analysis of Food Products
  • Nutritional composition
  • Additives and preservatives
  • Chemical contaminants
  • Heavy metals in food
🍽 Drinks, meat, dairy, fruits, processed food
WCL
Water Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Water Quality Testing
  • Drinking water analysis
  • Surface and groundwater testing
  • Wastewater monitoring
  • Ice quality testing
💧 Ensure safe water for public use
NFCL
Non-Food Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Non-Food Chemical Products
  • Cosmetics testing
  • Cleaning agents analysis
  • Plastics and materials
  • Industrial products
🧪 Ensure safety and regulatory compliance
MEEL
Mechanical Electrical Electronics Lab
Focus: Engineering & Electronic Testing
  • Electrical equipment testing
  • Mechanical safety testing
  • Electronic device evaluation
⚡ Product certification and industrial standards
REL
Research Laboratory
Focus: Innovation & Advanced Research
  • Scientific research projects
  • Method development
  • New technology testing
🔭 Support long-term innovation and development
