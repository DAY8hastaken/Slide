Online Service Workflow

From sample submission to certified report delivery — our streamlined 10-step process ensures accuracy, traceability, and transparency at every stage.

1
Customer submits application with sample details
2
Customer service verifies request & information
3
Sample ID assigned & invoice issued
4
Customer completes payment
5
Sample received & registered
6
Sample distributed to appropriate laboratory
7
Laboratory performs testing & analysis
8
Results reviewed & validated
9
Certificate generated with QR code
10
Results delivered to customer

Organizational Strategy

Six Strategic Pillars

STINL's development is guided by six interconnected strategies, each addressing a critical dimension of sustainable laboratory excellence.

I
👥
Human Resources & Organization
Focus on workforce development and building internal capability through structured training programs and organizational improvement.
Staff Evaluation Training Capacity Building Workplace Safety
Goal: Build a strong, skilled, and sustainable workforce.
II
📈
Business & Market Development
Ensure financial sustainability and growth through strategic planning, market expansion, and creation of innovative new services.
Financial Management Forecasting Market Expansion
Goal: Maintain revenue growth and competitiveness.
III
⚙️
Operational Excellence
Improve efficiency and reliability of daily operations through standard procedures, quality control, and robust asset and logistics management.
SOP Quality Control Asset Management Logistics
Goal: Deliver consistent and high-quality services.
IV
💻
Digital & IT Management
Drive digital transformation through modern website development, integrated data management systems, and a one-stop ERP service platform.
Website Dev Data Systems ERP One-Stop Platform
Goal: Improve accessibility, efficiency, and transparency.
V
🔬
Technical Capability Development
Strengthen laboratory scientific capacity by expanding testing parameters, increasing throughput, and pursuing ISO 17025 / 17043 accreditation.
ISO 17025 ISO 17043 Reference Labs Test Expansion
Goal: Achieve international-level technical standards.
VI
🤝
Partnerships & Collaboration
Enhance cooperation with external stakeholders across public-private partnerships, national collaborations, and international cooperation networks.
Public-Private National International
Goal: Promote innovation and knowledge exchange.
Our Facilities

Specialized Laboratories

STINL comprises six purpose-built laboratory divisions, each equipped to deliver specialized testing and analysis to international standards.

MBL
Microbiology Laboratory
Focus: Microorganism Testing
  • Detection of bacteria and pathogens
  • Food safety testing
  • Water microbiology
  • Public health monitoring
⚕ Prevent disease and contamination risks
FCL
Food Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Chemical Analysis of Food Products
  • Nutritional composition
  • Additives and preservatives
  • Chemical contaminants
  • Heavy metals in food
🍽 Drinks, meat, dairy, fruits, processed food
WCL
Water Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Water Quality Testing
  • Drinking water analysis
  • Surface and groundwater testing
  • Wastewater monitoring
  • Ice quality testing
💧 Ensure safe water for public use
NFCL
Non-Food Chemistry Laboratory
Focus: Non-Food Chemical Products
  • Cosmetics testing
  • Cleaning agents analysis
  • Plastics and materials
  • Industrial products
🧪 Ensure safety and regulatory compliance
MEEL
Mechanical Electrical Electronics Lab
Focus: Engineering & Electronic Testing
  • Electrical equipment testing
  • Mechanical safety testing
  • Electronic device evaluation
⚡ Product certification and industrial standards
REL
Research Laboratory
Focus: Innovation & Advanced Research
  • Scientific research projects
  • Method development
  • New technology testing
🔭 Support long-term innovation and development
